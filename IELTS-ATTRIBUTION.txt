Adaptations: extracted single-word English headwords and their Chinese glosses, combined and deduplicated selected glosses, sorted, and converted to TSV; the built-in subset retains source CEFR levels B1–C2. The optional TSV contains all supported levels. Phrases and punctuated abbreviations are not included because the subtitle matcher currently matches individual words.
The source describes its text as AI-generated and not manually proofread entry by entry. This is an unofficial IELTS preparation list; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
